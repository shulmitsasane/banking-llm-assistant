LOAN APPLICATION AND PROCESSING GUIDE

1. Overview

A loan application is a request from a customer to borrow funds from the bank.

The bank may evaluate the application using customer information, income details, repayment ability, credit information, and internal lending rules.

2. Common Loan Types

Examples may include:

- Personal loan
- Home loan
- Vehicle loan
- Education loan
- Business loan

3. Basic Application Information

A loan application may include:

- Customer ID
- Full name
- Date of birth
- Address
- Employment status
- Employer name
- Monthly income
- Existing financial obligations
- Requested loan amount
- Loan tenure
- Loan purpose

4. Supporting Documents

Supporting documents may include:

- Identity proof
- Address proof
- Income proof
- Salary slips
- Bank statements
- Employment information
- Tax documents
- Property or asset documents where applicable

5. Loan Application Process

A typical loan process may involve:

1. Customer submits the application.
2. System checks mandatory fields.
3. Customer documents are validated.
4. Eligibility checks are performed.
5. Credit information is reviewed.
6. Repayment capacity is evaluated.
7. Application is reviewed.
8. Decision is recorded.
9. Approved loan proceeds may be disbursed.

6. Application Statuses

Common statuses may include:

- Draft
- Submitted
- Under Review
- Additional Information Required
- Approved
- Rejected
- Disbursed
- Cancelled

7. Mandatory Field Validation

The system should prevent submission if mandatory information is missing.

Examples include:

- Missing customer ID
- Missing income information
- Missing requested loan amount
- Missing employment details
- Missing supporting documents

8. Eligibility Review

Eligibility may depend on:

- Customer age
- Employment status
- Income
- Existing financial obligations
- Requested amount
- Loan tenure
- Credit information
- Internal policy

9. Repayment Capacity

Repayment capacity refers to the customer's ability to make the required loan payments.

The system may consider:

- Monthly income
- Existing monthly obligations
- Loan instalment amount
- Other debts

10. Sample Error Codes

LN101 - Mandatory application information is missing.

LN102 - Customer identity verification is incomplete.

LN103 - Income information could not be validated.

LN104 - Requested loan amount exceeds configured eligibility.

LN105 - Required supporting documents are missing.

LN106 - Application is already under review.

LN107 - Application could not be submitted because of inconsistent data.

11. Resolution Guidance

For LN101:
Review all mandatory fields and complete the missing information.

For LN102:
Complete customer verification before continuing.

For LN103:
Verify income information and supporting documents.

For LN104:
Review the requested amount and configured eligibility.

For LN105:
Upload the required supporting documents.

For LN106:
Do not create a duplicate application. Check the existing application status.

For LN107:
Review the entered customer, employment, and financial information for inconsistencies.

12. Application Review

A reviewer may check:

- Completeness of information
- Customer verification status
- Income and employment information
- Credit information
- Existing obligations
- Requested amount
- Supporting documents

13. Rejection

A rejected application should include a recorded reason or status according to the system's business process.

Support staff should not manually change a rejected application to approved without following the proper approval process.

14. Disbursement

After approval, a loan may proceed to disbursement.

Before disbursement, the system may verify:

- Approval status
- Customer account
- Loan amount
- Required documents
- Final authorization

15. Common Support Questions

Customers or staff may ask:

- Why is the loan application still under review?
- What documents are missing?
- Why was the application not submitted?
- What is the current loan status?
- Why is the requested amount not eligible?
- Has the loan been disbursed?

16. Escalation

Escalation may be required when:

- Loan status is stuck
- Application data is inconsistent
- Document validation repeatedly fails
- Approved loan cannot be disbursed
- System displays an unexplained processing error
