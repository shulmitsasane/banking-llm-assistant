KYC AND CUSTOMER VERIFICATION GUIDE

1. Overview

KYC stands for Know Your Customer. KYC is the process used by banks and financial institutions to verify the identity of a customer before providing certain banking services.

The purpose of KYC is to confirm that the customer is genuine, ensure that customer records are accurate, and reduce the risk of fraud, identity misuse, and other financial crimes.

2. Basic Customer Information

The bank may collect information such as:

- Full name
- Date of birth
- Residential address
- Mobile number
- Email address
- PAN number
- Aadhaar number or another identity reference
- Occupation
- Employment details
- Source of income

3. Common Identity Documents

Common documents used for identity verification may include:

- PAN card
- Aadhaar card
- Passport
- Driving licence
- Voter ID

The exact set of accepted documents may depend on the bank's internal policy and the type of account being opened.

4. Address Verification

The customer may also be required to provide address proof.

Examples may include:

- Aadhaar card
- Passport
- Utility bill
- Bank statement
- Registered rent agreement
- Driving licence

The address entered in the banking system should match the document submitted by the customer.

5. KYC Validation Process

A typical KYC process may include the following steps:

1. Customer submits personal details.
2. Identity and address documents are uploaded or provided.
3. The system checks whether mandatory fields are complete.
4. The documents are reviewed for validity.
5. Customer information is matched with the submitted documents.
6. Any mismatch is flagged for review.
7. Once verification is completed, the KYC status is updated.

6. KYC Statuses

The system may maintain statuses such as:

- Pending
- Under Review
- Verified
- Rejected
- Expired
- Update Required

7. Common KYC Issues

Common issues may include:

- Missing PAN number
- Invalid document format
- Expired passport or licence
- Name mismatch
- Address mismatch
- Incomplete date of birth
- Blurred or unreadable document
- Duplicate customer record

8. Sample Error Codes

KYC101 - Mandatory identity information is missing.

KYC102 - Customer name does not match the submitted identity document.

KYC103 - Address information is incomplete or inconsistent.

KYC104 - Submitted identity document has expired.

KYC105 - Uploaded document is unreadable.

KYC106 - Possible duplicate customer record detected.

9. Suggested Resolution Steps

For KYC101:
Check all mandatory fields and request the missing customer information.

For KYC102:
Compare the customer name entered in the system with the identity document and correct any spelling or formatting differences.

For KYC103:
Verify the residential address and request updated address proof if required.

For KYC104:
Request a valid, non-expired document.

For KYC105:
Ask the customer to upload a clearer copy.

For KYC106:
Check whether the customer already has an existing profile before creating a new customer record.

10. KYC Update

Customers may be required to update KYC when:

- A document expires
- The customer's address changes
- The customer changes their legal name
- The bank requests periodic re-verification
- Existing customer data is incomplete

11. Escalation

If the system cannot automatically verify a customer's KYC details, the case may be sent for manual review.

Manual review may be required when:

- Documents contain inconsistent information
- Duplicate customer records are suspected
- Identity verification fails repeatedly
- Customer information cannot be validated

12. Support Guidance

A support employee should not approve or modify KYC information without following the required process.

If sufficient information is not available, the user should be informed that the case requires further verification or escalation.
