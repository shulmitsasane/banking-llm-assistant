FIXED DEPOSIT OPERATIONS GUIDE

1. Overview

A fixed deposit is a banking product in which a customer deposits a specified amount for a predefined period.

The deposit earns interest based on the agreed interest rate and tenure.

2. Common Fixed Deposit Information

A fixed deposit record may contain:

- Customer ID
- Deposit account number
- Principal amount
- Interest rate
- Start date
- Maturity date
- Tenure
- Nominee information
- Maturity instructions
- Current deposit status

3. Fixed Deposit Creation

A typical process may include:

1. Customer selects deposit amount.
2. Customer chooses tenure.
3. System displays applicable interest rate.
4. Customer confirms maturity instructions.
5. Source account is debited.
6. Fixed deposit account is created.
7. Maturity date is generated.

4. Deposit Statuses

Common statuses may include:

- Active
- Matured
- Closed
- Prematurely Closed
- Renewal Pending
- Renewed

5. Maturity

The maturity date is the date on which the agreed fixed deposit tenure ends.

At maturity, the bank may:

- Credit principal and interest to the customer's account
- Renew the fixed deposit
- Follow another maturity instruction configured by the customer

6. Premature Closure

Premature closure means closing the fixed deposit before the maturity date.

The customer may receive:

- Principal amount
- Interest calculated according to premature closure rules
- Amount after any applicable penalty

7. Premature Closure Checks

Before premature closure, the system should verify:

- Deposit is active
- Customer is authorized
- Deposit is eligible for premature closure
- Applicable penalty or reduced interest rate
- Final payable amount

8. Sample Error Codes

FD101 - Fixed deposit account not found.

FD102 - Deposit is already closed.

FD103 - Premature closure is not allowed for this deposit product.

FD104 - Deposit has already matured.

FD105 - Customer authorization failed.

FD106 - Unable to calculate final settlement amount.

9. Error Resolution

For FD101:
Verify the fixed deposit account number.

For FD102:
Check the closure history before processing another request.

For FD103:
Review the deposit product rules.

For FD104:
Follow the maturity process instead of premature closure.

For FD105:
Verify customer ownership and authorization.

For FD106:
Escalate if the system cannot determine the final payable amount.

10. Renewal

A fixed deposit may be renewed automatically or manually.

Renewal may apply to:

- Principal only
- Principal plus interest

The new deposit may use the interest rate applicable on the renewal date.

11. Customer Support Questions

Common questions include:

- What is my maturity date?
- What happens if I close the deposit early?
- Why is the final amount lower than expected?
- Can I renew my deposit?
- What happens at maturity?

12. Escalation

Escalation may be required when:

- Deposit amount is incorrect
- Interest calculation appears incorrect
- Deposit status does not update
- Maturity payment is not credited
- Premature closure fails repeatedly
